This policy is part of the Terms of Service. It explains how to cancel, what happens to your credits, and when money is returned.

1. Cancelling

You can cancel a subscription at any time from the account panel inside EasyField, or by writing to [support@easyfield.ai].

Cancelling stops the next renewal. It does not end the period you have already paid for — you keep access, and your remaining credits for that period, until it ends. We do not pro-rate a partial period unless one of the situations below applies.

2. Statutory right to withdraw

[EU/UK CONSUMER WITHDRAWAL — consumers ordinarily have 14 days to withdraw from a distance contract. For digital content supplied immediately there is a specific regime: the right can be lost only if the consumer gave express prior consent to immediate supply AND acknowledged losing the right. The lawyer must confirm the exact wording and whether the checkout flow captures that acknowledgement — if it does not, the 14-day right survives in full and this section must say so.]

Where this right applies, it takes precedence over everything else in this policy.

3. Discretionary refunds

We will normally refund, on request within [14 DAYS] of the charge, when:

  • you were charged twice for the same period;
  • you were charged after cancelling;
  • a fault on our side prevented you from using the service for a material part of the period and we could not fix it;
  • you were charged for a renewal you did not intend and have not used the new period's credits.

We will normally not refund when:

  • the period's credits have been substantially used;
  • generated output did not meet your expectations — generative models are inherently variable, and a run that completed consumed real provider cost;
  • you no longer have a compatible Mac or a licensed copy of DaVinci Resolve Studio;
  • your account was terminated for a breach of the Terms.

Where a refund is given for a period, any credits granted for that period are removed. If they have already been spent, we may refund only the unused portion.

4. Failed generations

You are not charged for a run that fails. Credits are reserved when a run starts and released automatically if it does not complete. If you believe you were charged for a failed run, write to [support@easyfield.ai] with the date and the tool you used and we will check the ledger and correct it.

5. The Partner lifetime purchase

Partner is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and it does not include credits. Refunds on it are governed by section 2 where a statutory right applies. Outside that, [STATE THE POLICY EXPLICITLY — a lifetime product needs a clear, deliberate refund window, and the entitlement is revoked when a refund or chargeback is processed].

6. How to ask

Write to [support@easyfield.ai] with the email address on the account and the approximate date of the charge. We aim to reply within [2 BUSINESS DAYS] and to decide within [10 BUSINESS DAYS]. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method; how long the money takes to appear depends on your bank.

7. Chargebacks

If something has gone wrong, please contact us before asking your bank to reverse the charge — it is almost always faster. A chargeback ends the entitlement it paid for, including Partner lifetime access.
