2. What we collect

Account data

Your email address and a hashed password, created when you register. We never see your password in readable form. If you sign in with a third-party identity provider, we receive the email address and account identifier that provider releases to us.

Billing data

A record of your plan, your credit balance and ledger, and evidence of each payment. We do not receive or store your card number. Payment details are handled entirely by our payment provider, named in the sub-processor table below.

Content you choose to process

When you run a cloud tool, the material you selected for that run — which may include video frames, images, audio, and the text of your prompt — is uploaded so it can be processed. This is the most sensitive category of data EasyField handles, and it is worth being precise about it:

  • Nothing is uploaded until you start a run. Browsing the panel uploads nothing.
  • Your DaVinci Resolve project file is never uploaded. Only the media you select for a run leaves your Mac.
  • Three tools run entirely on your own machine and send nothing at all: Animations, Transcribe and Beat Detection.
  • If the material you select contains other people — their faces, voices or likenesses — you are responsible for having the rights to process it. See the Terms of Service.

Technical data

Our servers record ordinary request logs (IP address, timestamp, endpoint) as a necessary part of operating and securing the service. The installed plugin does not currently collect analytics, diagnostics or crash reports. 8. Security

Passwords are stored hashed. Access to the database is restricted by row-level security so one customer's records are not reachable from another's session. Authentication tokens stay in the plugin's main process and are not exposed to the panel's interface layer. Passwords must be at least 12 characters.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant supervisory authority where the law requires it.
